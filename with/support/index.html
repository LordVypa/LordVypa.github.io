Frequently Asked Questions

What platforms will Whisper in the Halls be available on?

The game will launch on PC (Windows) via Steam. Additional platform support has not been announced.

When does the game release?

A release date has not been announced yet. Wishlist the game on Steam to be notified the moment it goes live.

The game won't launch — what do I do?

Try verifying the game files through Steam (right-click the game → Properties → Local Files → Verify integrity). If the issue persists, email us at support@freakganginteractive.com with your system specs and the Steam error message.

I'm experiencing crashes or freezes mid-game.

Please email us with your OS version, GPU model, and a description of when the crash occurs. If you have a crash log, attach it — it helps us fix the issue faster for everyone.

My save file is missing or corrupted.

We're sorry to hear that. Email us and we'll do our best to help recover your progress. Include your Steam username and approximately how far you were in the game.

Does the game contain content warnings?

Whisper in the Halls deals with themes of institutional trauma, child neglect, and psychological horror. Detailed content warnings will be listed on the Steam store page prior to release.

I have a question not covered here.

Just email us at support@freakganginteractive.com — we're happy to help.
